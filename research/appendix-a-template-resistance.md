# Appendix A: Template Resistance Notes

The AGP play template was used unchanged so that resistance could be measured
rather than pre-empted. Observations from drafting the current batch of plays.

## 1. No slot for evidence status

The template assumes a play carries its own evidence, which held in AGP where
practices and outcome data arrived in the same paper. Here, most plays have no
outcome evidence at all. Evidence status is currently recorded as prose in the
Source section by convention, which is a workaround rather than structure.

## 2. No slot for capability assumptions or freshness

The Mollick plays are built around a March 2023 distinction between models with
and without live search access, a distinction that has since largely dissolved.
The practices still work; their stated assumptions are dated, and there is
nowhere in the template to say so. This is the most likely first structural
revision.

## 3. Examples quality tracks source type sharply

Where the source is a paper containing actual prompts and deployment variants,
Examples is rich and concrete. Where the source is a teaching-centre bullet
list, Examples is thin or nearly empty. Plays 7 and 8 are the weakest sections
in the collection for exactly this reason, and no amount of drafting fixes it
without inventing detail. The template presumes a source depth this domain does
not reliably supply.

## 4. Problem sections strain

In AGP the problem is typically an internal failure of an existing grading
practice. Here the problem is frequently an external condition, namely that
students have access to generative AI, rather than a flaw in what the
instructor was doing. The section fills, but the rhetorical shape differs and
several Problems read as situation description rather than diagnosis.

## 5. The Source section is carrying four jobs

Citation, paired sourcing, drafted-step labelling, and citation corrections are
all landing in one field. In this batch, Source is frequently longer than
References and occasionally longer than Intent.

## 6. No slot marking which implementation steps were drafted

Plays 7 and 8 required inline markers plus a prose preamble to distinguish
sourced from drafted steps. This was the most-used workaround in the batch and
the one most likely to be applied inconsistently by a different author.

## 7. Batch two confirmed observation 3 and sharpened it

Plays 12 to 16 are sourced from teaching-centre pages that give one to three
sentences per practice. Every one of their Examples sections is built from the
source's framing rather than from worked instances, because no worked instances
exist in the sources. Across sixteen plays the pattern is now unambiguous:
Examples is the section this domain cannot reliably fill, and the template
offers no alternative for a practice that is real but undocumented in detail.

## 8. Drafted-step density is rising

Plays 1 to 5 required no drafted steps. Plays 12 to 16 average two drafted steps
each, and Play 16 required three of seven. As mining moves from the one
well-documented paper toward the broad guidance literature, the proportion of
the play the drafter is writing goes up. The template has no way to signal this
at a glance, so a reader cannot tell a fully sourced play from a mostly drafted
one without reading the Source section.

## 9. Examples is not in the published AGP template

Observation added during repository construction, not present in the original
draft. The AGP play template at
`_chapters/900-how-to-contribute/200-play-template.md` in the AGP repository has
nine sections and **Examples is not one of them**: Intent, Problem, Solution,
Applicability, How to Implement, See Also, Source, References, Community
Discussion. No AGP play in the repository uses an Examples heading.

All sixteen plays in this collection have one. So the claim that the template
was used unchanged holds for the other eight sections but not for this one, and
observations 3 and 7 above are findings about a section this collection added
rather than about a section AGP supplied. This needs resolving before the
finding is written up: either the drafting worked from a different template
version than the one published, or Examples was an unrecorded addition. The
repository ships the AGP template byte-identical, so the two are currently out
of step.
